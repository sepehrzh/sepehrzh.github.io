Sepehr Zhand, Ph.D.

Geospatial data scientist for urban systems

A UK-Based geospatial data scientist working at the interface of academia, industry, and policy, I design scalable analytical frameworks for complex urban networks and translate them into production-grade decision-support tools. My path began with development planning alongside marginalised communities in Iran and India; a foundation that now informs research at UCL and LSE and consultancy work with municipal authorities across Europe, Africa, and Australia.

I specialise in modelling population, inequality, transport, and land-use dynamics at scale, from continental spatial network pipelines on Google Cloud Platform to interactive dashboards adopted directly by city governments, and bring this cross-sector, field-informed perspective to teaching and research supervision.
